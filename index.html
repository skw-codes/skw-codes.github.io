01 / About

Partnerships that turn into revenue.

I have spent my career working across the full partner spectrum: hardware OEMs, global system integrators, Tier 1 operators, distributors and managed service providers. I started as a sales engineer in Sri Lanka, spent seven years at BlackBerry, and moved into alliance leadership at MobileIron, NetMotion and Absolute Security.

Today I lead EMEA and India alliances, connecting commercial strategy with practical execution: choosing the right partners, building a credible route to market, and giving teams the structure to deliver.

How I work

Qualify for fit, not size.

The biggest partner is not always the right partner. I test whether the business model fits before committing resource.

Build the arithmetic before the argument.

Use models, assumptions and evidence to win support, then prove ideas lean before asking for investment.

Use credible doors.

Existing OEM relationships can create a more effective path into large integrators and new markets.

Lead through teams.

Recruit, coach and retain strong people while building trust across regional and global teams.

Education: BSc (Hons) Computing and Information Systems, London Metropolitan University.
